# AI Video Workflow Case Study — MIT SDE Ad

### 1. Objective

Create a 45–60 sec AI-generated ad targeting working professionals (25–35) who feel stuck in career growth.
Goal: Show problem → introduce solution → end with a clear, believable close.

###2. Workflow Overview

Flow: Idea → Prompt → Script → Refinement → Video → Iteration

### Tools used:

ChatGPT — Idea and script generation
Grok AI — variations
HeyGen — avatar video generation

### 5. creating script 

**version 1 without refinement:**

Create a short ad script for MIT SDE.

The script should be about a working professional who is not getting salary growth and then improves after joining MIT SDE.

Use the hook line: "Salary badh nahi rahi… ya skills?"

Keep it under 30 seconds and make it emotional.

**version 2 with refinement:**

Act as a professional ad scriptwriter.

Create a 25–30 second storytelling-style ad script for MIT SDE.

Target audience:
Working professionals (25–35 years old) who feel stuck in their career despite having experience.

Objective:
Highlight the frustration of slow salary growth and connect it to the need for upgrading skills.

Hook line:
"Salary badh nahi rahi… ya skills?"

Tone:
Friendly, relatable, slightly emotional (not overly casual or salesy)

Style:
Single-person narrative (first-person storytelling)

Constraints:
- Use simple Hinglish language
- Keep sentences short and natural
- Add pauses (...) after important phrases for better delivery
- Make it suitable for AI avatar narration

Structure:
1. Hook (pain-driven, attention-grabbing)
2. Personal struggle (relatable situation)
3. Realization (skills gap insight)
4. Subtle introduction of MIT SDE
5. Realistic closing (no exaggeration)

Final instruction:
Ensure the script feels human, believable, and not like a typical advertisement.



### 7. Output — prompt + script V2 (Final)

Create a vertical 9:16 short video optimized for Instagram Reels and Stories using this exact script. Use a friendly, confident Indian male avatar in his early 30s, wearing a smart casual blue shirt. Modern, clean office background with subtle depth. Warm, premium lighting.

Script:
"Salary badh nahi rahi… ya skills?
Main bhi wahi tha. Office, targets, aur roz ek hi jagah atak gaya tha. Promotion nahi, increment nahi… bas time guzar raha tha.
Phir Maine MIT SDE ke online PGDM program join kiya. AICTE approved, bilkul working professionals ke liye bana hua. Marketing, Finance, Supply Chain… jo bhi chahiye, practical skills aur real industry exposure.
Aaj skills badhe hain… toh opportunities khud badh rahi hain.
MIT SDE. Skills jo salary ko badhaayein.
Ab aapki baari hai. Join karo… aur aage badho."
Style & Delivery Instructions:

Tone: Friendly, conversational, and motivational yet professional.
Pace: Natural storytelling speed with clear pauses after key lines for better impact.
Emphasize: “ya skills”, “atak gaya tha”, “skills badhe hain”, and “MIT SDE”.
Use natural head movements and subtle hand gestures.
Camera: Vertical medium close-up, eye-level, centered framing.
On-screen text: Elegantly display key phrases like “Salary badh nahi rahi… ya skills?” and “MIT SDE” when spoken.
Add subtle, uplifting background music that remains soft under the voice.
Video duration: Strictly under 30 seconds.
Aspect Ratio: 9:16 (Vertical)

Generate in 1080x1920 resolution, high quality.

### 10. Key Learnings
Prompt structure directly impacts output quality
Iteration is essential—first output is rarely usable
Different tools behave differently:
ChatGPT → structured output
Grok AI → more conversational tone
Small changes (hook, pauses) create major impact

# AI Video Workflow Case Study — MIT SDE Ad

## 1. Objective
Create a 30–45 sec AI-generated ad targeting working professionals (25–35) facing stagnant career growth.

Goal:  
Problem → Insight → Solution → Realistic Close

---

## 2. Workflow Overview

**Flow:**  
Idea → Prompt → Script → Refinement → Video → Iteration

**Tools Used:**
- ChatGPT — Idea generation and structured scripts  
- Grok AI — Tone refinement and variations  
- HeyGen — Avatar-based video generation  

---

## 3. Hook Exploration

Generated multiple hook ideas using AI.

**Selection Criteria:**
- Relatability  
- Emotional trigger  
- Scroll-stopping ability  

**Selected Hook:**  
**“Salary badh nahi rahi… ya skills?”**

**Reason:**  
Directly triggers self-reflection and targets a real pain point for working professionals.

---

## 4. Script Generation

### Version 1 (Basic Prompt)
Create a short ad script for MIT SDE.  
The script should be about a working professional who is not getting salary growth and then improves after joining MIT SDE.  
Use the hook line: "Salary badh nahi rahi… ya skills?"  
Keep it under 30 seconds and make it emotional.

---

### Issues in Version 1
- Generic output  
- Lack of structure  
- Slightly robotic tone  
- No control over delivery style  

---

### Version 2 (Refined Prompt)
Act as a professional ad scriptwriter.

Create a 25–30 second storytelling-style ad script for MIT SDE.

**Target audience:**  
Working professionals (25–35 years old) who feel stuck in their career despite having experience.

**Objective:**  
Highlight frustration of slow salary growth and connect it to the need for upgrading skills.

**Hook line:**  
"Salary badh nahi rahi… ya skills?"

**Tone:**  
Friendly, relatable, slightly emotional (not overly casual or salesy)

**Style:**  
Single-person narrative (first-person storytelling)

**Constraints:**
- Use simple Hinglish language  
- Keep sentences short and natural  
- Add pauses (...) after important phrases  
- Make it suitable for AI avatar narration  

**Structure:**
1. Hook  
2. Personal struggle  
3. Realization  
4. Subtle MIT SDE mention  
5. Realistic closing  

**Final Instruction:**  
Ensure the script feels human, believable, and not like a typical advertisement.

---

## 5. Final Video Prompt & Script (V2)

Create a vertical 9:16 short video optimized for Instagram Reels and Stories.

**Avatar:** Friendly, confident Indian male (early 30s), smart casual  
**Background:** Modern office, clean aesthetic  
**Lighting:** Warm, premium  

**Script:**
"Salary badh nahi rahi… ya skills?  
Main bhi wahi tha. Office, targets… aur roz ek hi jagah atak gaya tha.  
Promotion nahi, increment nahi… bas time guzar raha tha.  

Phir maine MIT SDE ke online PGDM program join kiya… job ke saath hi.  
AICTE approved… aur practical learning pe focus.  

Aaj skills badhe hain… toh opportunities khud badh rahi hain.  

MIT SDE. Skills jo salary ko badhaayein.  
Ab aapki baari hai."

**Delivery Instructions:**
- Tone: Conversational, natural  
- Pace: Slightly slow with pauses  
- Emphasis: “ya skills”, “atak gaya tha”, “skills badhe hain”  
- Camera: Vertical, medium close-up  
- Add subtle background music  

**Output:** 1080x1920, under 30 sec

---

## 6. Output Evaluation

**Improvements:**
- Stronger, pain-driven hook  
- More natural delivery due to pauses  
- Better emotional connection  
- Reduced salesy tone  

**Limitations:**
- Ending visual can be stronger  
- Emotional depth can be improved further  

---

## 7. Key Learnings

- Prompt structure directly impacts output quality  
- Iteration is essential for improvement  
- Different tools produce different styles:
  - ChatGPT → structured output  
  - Grok AI → conversational tone  
- Small changes (hook, pauses) create significant impact  

---

## 8. Conclusion

This project demonstrates:
- Structured prompt design  
- Output evaluation and refinement  
- End-to-end AI video workflow execution  
- Practical use of multiple AI tools  

It highlights the importance of iteration in achieving high-quality AI-generated content.
